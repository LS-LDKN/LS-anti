Target Audience & Objective

  • Campaign Tagline: "Wear the Story. Not the Landfill."
  • Target Audience: Gen-Z and Millennials (18–30 years) who frequently purchase low-cost fast-fashion clothing online.
  • Communication Objective: Expose the hidden ecological price of disposable clothing and prompt a shift toward rewearing and circular fashion without guilt-tripping.

AI Tools & Workflow Used

  • 1. Ideation & Copywriting (Gemini): Formulated the core concept "The Hidden Receipt", drafted taglines, audience persona, and caption copy.
  • 2. Image Generation (Midjourney / Firefly): Created visual assets using targeted camera directions (macro clothing tag vs. editorial studio gown).
  • 3. Layout & Assembly (Canva / Figma): Combined typography, visual elements, negative space alignment, and cohesive color palette for final export.
