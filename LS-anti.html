03. Strategic Architecture
Official Tagline
"Wear the Story. Not the Landfill."
Target Audience
Urban Gen-Z and Millennials (Ages 18–30) frequently shopping micro-trend drops and low-cost fast-fashion platforms.
Communication Objective
Deconstruct impulse-buying psychology by visually tying cheap fast garments directly to physical landfill waste, transforming mindful re-wearing into an aspirational flex.
